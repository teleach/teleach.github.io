I'm a quantitative analyst with a PhD in Financial Technologies and around six years of applied experience, four on the buy-side, with earlier research roles at the Bank for International Settlements, the European Central Bank and R3.

Most recently I worked at a digital asset fund on portfolio analytics and research. Before that my research covered digital money, payments, and cyber and operational risk.

I've worked with a range of methods across econometrics, statistics and data science. Some of that work is published in peer-reviewed journals (see [Publications](/publications/)).

**Tools:** Python, SQL, R · machine learning and time-series modelling · LLM and RAG prototyping · Docker, Linux

**Languages:** English (native) · Italian (C1) · German (B1)
