I'm a quantitative analyst with over six years of applied work in buy-side portfolio analytics and central-bank research. I build the models, data pipelines and Python tooling that risk and investment decisions run on.

I currently work in portfolio analytics and research at a digital asset fund, building Python tooling for NAV reconciliation, VaR/CVaR risk reporting and factor decomposition of portfolio returns. Before that I did research at the Bank for International Settlements, the European Central Bank and R3, on digital money, payments, and cyber and operational risk.

On the methods side I work with survival analysis and time-to-event models, hierarchical Bayesian inference, and simulation-based risk quantification on large event datasets. Some of that work is published in peer-reviewed journals (see [Publications](/publications/)).

**Tools:** Python, SQL, R · machine learning and time-series modelling · LLM and RAG prototyping · Docker, Linux

**Languages:** English (native) · Italian (C1) · German (B1)

**Education:** PhD in Financial Technologies, University of Pavia
