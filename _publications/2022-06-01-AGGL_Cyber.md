Using a database of more than 100,000 cyber events across sectors, we document what makes cyber incidents costly. Costs are higher for larger firms and for incidents that hit several organisations at once. The financial sector faces more attacks but lower average costs, consistent with its higher IT security spending. Cloud use is associated with lower costs, especially for smaller incidents, while crypto-related activities are particularly exposed.
