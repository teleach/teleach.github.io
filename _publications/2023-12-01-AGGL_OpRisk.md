Using cross-country, event-level data on more than 500,000 operational losses at banks (2002–2018), we document how operational risk has evolved. Operational value-at-risk ranges from 6% to 12% of gross income depending on the estimation method, with a growing cyber component. Losses take more than a year on average to be discovered and recognised. Operational losses depend on macroeconomic and regulatory conditions: very accommodative monetary policy is followed by higher losses, and stronger supervision is associated with lower ones.
